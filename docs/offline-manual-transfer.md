# Review and copy offline tracker data

The device-only **Continue offline** tracker is separate from signed-in workspaces. It does not automatically sync. To transfer it, sign in on the same browser/device, open the destination (Personal or a team), then open **Settings → Data → Review offline changes**. For a team, wait for online, server-confirmed membership and a clean, confirmed team snapshot; reviewers cannot copy financial records into the team ledger.

The review lists each added record and each same-type/same-ID conflict. Expand a record to see its fields and local images. Choose **current workspace** or **offline** for every conflict; the copy is unavailable until all conflicts are resolved and the resulting supported financial links validate. Confirming copies the selected records without deleting source or destination-only records. A second review skips identical records, making retries safe for the same IDs. Records with different IDs can still be duplicates: inspect carefully. Organization details, settings, deleted records, team request history and external Firestore documents are not transferred. This is a one-way copy, not a two-way or automatic synchronization.

A selected offline record, or destination record selected for replacement, with a shared-receipt reference or pending approval/deletion is blocked: those refer to workspace-specific cloud workflows and cannot safely be recreated by copying a snapshot. Refund records with embedded local receipt images are blocked when copying to a team, because this transfer cannot yet publish them as shared receipts. Other embedded images remain device-local when copied to a team; they are not uploaded. The review handles at most 500 changed/conflicting records, and its validation covers supported schemas and relationships, not the truth or completeness of financial data.

The offline copy is never deleted. The application checks the source and destination again after confirmation and stops if the account, workspace, access, data or team baseline changed. Team copies are first saved locally and queued through the existing revision-checked sync; the success message says to wait for team sync, not that the server has confirmed it. A conflict or failure requires existing team-draft recovery and manual reconciliation; keep the offline data and independent verified backups. Browser storage can fail or be cleared. Tests include synthetic local storage and mocked team UI plus existing Firestore sync tests; real devices, deployment, multiple-user races, quotas and failure recovery need staging verification.

## Continuing during team draft recovery

When the team recovery warning is shown, **Continue offline** opens the existing separate device-only tracker, not the team snapshot or the unconfirmed draft. A one-time confirmation explains this. The app checks that the same draft is readable, local writes have settled, no team push is in flight, and the active Pondo service worker has the matching app shell cached before it reloads. If it cannot verify those conditions, it stays on the current page and advises downloading a backup; it never discards the draft. Finish any unsaved forms before switching. A device-stored draft is not an independent backup and may be lost if browser storage is cleared or evicted.

Use **Team & draft** to download the draft. Returning to sign-in does not automatically reconcile it. After reconnecting, choose **Reconcile draft** in the team recovery warning or Team panel. This reviews the saved team draft separately from the offline tracker; the Settings offline-data review does not import the team draft.


## Reconciling an unconfirmed team draft

Reconciliation requires an internet connection and server-confirmed editor membership. Pondo fetches the current shared snapshot and the saved local draft. Choose add or skip for each draft-only ID: it may have been deleted from the team after the draft was made. Choose the team or draft version for each conflict. Team-only IDs are preserved; changes to organization details, settings and deletions are not applied. The app validates the combined supported financial records before a revision-checked transaction; if the team copy, membership, local draft or workspace changes, review again.

Pending approvals, deletion requests, shared-receipt references, and local refund receipt images cannot be added or replaced by this path. Keep the team version or skip those additions and handle them separately. Other embedded images remain device-only. Financial validation checks supported structure and links, not correctness, completeness, privacy or duplicate records with different IDs.

Before sending, the app keeps a verified copy of the original draft in this browser’s localStorage. After server confirmation and a successful local save it clears the active recovery marker and reconnects the team view. **Download last reconciled draft** in Team retrieves the most recent device copy. Copies may accumulate, storage can fill or be cleared, and they are not independent backups. On error or an unconfirmed response, the active draft stays in place; the server may still have applied a write, so review against the server again before retrying. Tested with mocked transactions and browser UI; production rules, live multi-user concurrency, device storage and network failure need staging verification.
