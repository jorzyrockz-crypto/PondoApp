# Other Funds teacher payments

The Overview action records a payment with paid-by, purpose, amount, date and received-by. Team members are name suggestions, not identity verification; manually entered names are allowed. Purposes include Settle Leave of Absence and a manually entered alternative. After a successful local save, the app opens Funds / Other Funds, where the register shows its separate receipt balance and icon actions. Print produces payer and records copies using the Death Aid receipt layout. Handover requires an online team workspace, a non-sample payment and two distinct current members. Either participant may initiate a full-payment handover; the other must confirm or decline it. Declined requests can be retried with earlier decisions retained (up to 20 retries). A confirmed handover appears as Handed over and may be printed as an acknowledgment. Requests, decisions and retained declined attempts also appear in Transactions as no-cash-movement entries when the handover history is available. Requests and decisions do not change the fund balance. The balance is the sum of recorded payments only: it is not a regular fund, an available project allocation, a per-custodian balance, or evidence of cash being held or handed over. Edits retain an audit entry of the previous record. The unified Transactions history includes these as separately held, tracking-impact records, not income to existing funds.

Payments are saved in workspace snapshots, JSON backups, and the reviewed offline merge and team draft reconciliation. Older backups without this field are accepted; an overwrite with such a backup replaces the register with an empty one, so export and verify a current backup first. Team saves are local until cloud sync confirms them.

**Team rollout:** deploy the updated `firestore.rules` after reviewing them against the rules actually published for the correct Firebase project. The added rule prevents old clients from silently removing the `otherPayments` field once present. Until it is deployed, old app versions can erase the register on their next shared snapshot write. Update all team devices to v151 or later, close older tabs, and verify a backup before using this feature. Rules were changed in the repository only; this change does not publish them. A printout is not a verified signature or handover.

## Handover security and recovery

Handover documents are stored separately under `organizations/{org}/otherFundHandovers/{paymentId}`; they are not part of tracker snapshot JSON exports, offline transfer, or team-draft reconciliation. Keep an independent administrative backup of those Firestore documents before workspace recovery or data replacement. Do not delete them when restoring snapshots. Receipt copies cannot prove a physical transfer. Confirmations are tied to authenticated workspace accounts, not a typed payment name; the initial payment's received-by name is not an identity link.

Rules require both participants to be members, prevent the initiator from deciding, and require the confirmed payment snapshot to match the workspace payment at the time of confirmation. Older or otherwise authorized snapshot editors can still modify or remove the payment afterward: the register flags that discrepancy and the handover remains a historical record, not proof of the current balance or custody. The app blocks ordinary payment editing while its handover is pending or confirmed and while team handover status is unavailable, but this UI guard is not a server-enforced payment lock. The inbox lists the first 500 handover documents; missing statuses are treated as unavailable rather than assumed clear.

The updated rules in this repository must be reviewed and deployed to the intended Firebase project before team use. Run the Firestore emulator tests before deploying; local unit tests alone cannot establish the production security of this workflow. No rules or app deployment is performed automatically.
