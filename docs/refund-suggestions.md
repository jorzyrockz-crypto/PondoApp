# Refund form suggestions

The refund form suggests expense descriptions from saved refund records in the
current workspace. Person-to-reimburse suggestions come from previous refunds
and, in a confirmed team workspace, its members. Supplier and category
suggestions come from saved unpaid expenses and refunds in that workspace. The
shared unpaid-expense form also suggests suppliers and categories from those
records. Suggestions are optional: type a new value or select an offered one.
The existing signed-in name prefill remains editable.

Suggestions are derived from current records, deduplicated without regard to
case and limited to 100 values per field. They are refreshed when the form opens
or the field receives focus, and cleared on workspace transitions or while team
access/data are unconfirmed. They are not a separate saved directory. Selecting
text does not copy amounts, dates, reference numbers or attachments; recording a
refund still does not move fund cash. Names and expense details may be sensitive,
so use them only within the authorized workspace.
