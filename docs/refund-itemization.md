# Itemized refunds

A refund can be recorded as one total or itemized into up to 20 lines. Each line has a description, quantity (up to three decimal places), and a unit price (up to two decimal places). The line amount rounds half up to the nearest centavo; the refund total is the sum of the rounded lines. Itemized refunds remain a single payable and do not pay or reserve cash.

Item details are retained in the payable and, for reviewer submissions, in the immutable member-request inbox. The approval client validates the breakdown and checks that it equals the requested total before creating a payable; retries compare the full item data. The Firestore rules bound the serialized item field but cannot validate its internal arithmetic. Existing older clients do not validate or display these details, so approvers should update before reviewing itemized submissions. The accompanying Firestore rules must be deployed separately before reviewers can submit itemized requests.

Team refund image attachments are stored in separate immutable Firestore documents (up to 600,000 data URL characters), not in the ledger snapshot. The reviewer request and image are written together; editor refund images are written atomically with the team snapshot. The client verifies a SHA-256 digest before viewing or approving a member request. Images are converted to JPEG at a maximum dimension of 2,000 pixels; the user should download and zoom the preview to verify readability. If a clear image cannot fit, the app rejects it rather than silently compressing further. PDFs are not supported for shared refund receipts. Other expense attachments and legacy receipt images remain device-local.

Deploy the matching Firestore rules only after emulator and staging validation. Rules limit access to current workspace members, bound the attachment size and prevent receipt mutation, but cannot verify image contents or hashes. Older clients neither show nor verify shared receipts, and cannot enforce item arithmetic. Firestore receipt documents survive refund cancellation/rejection for history; backup exports do not include downloaded shared receipt documents, so JSON export alone is not a complete archive. Permanent workspace deletion and receipt cleanup are not yet implemented.
