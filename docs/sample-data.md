# Sample data preview

Settings → Data → **Load sample data** adds fictional, marked examples alongside the active workspace's records. Examples cover funds, projects and allocations, income and expenses, cash donation, supplier payable, paid and partially paid refunds, itemized refunds, a non-cash refund credit, fictional void history, regular and monthly contributions, project fundraising, and personal and fund-funded Death Aid with a recorded handover. Names and descriptions are labeled `[Sample]`. A generated receipt labeled SAMPLE is stored only on the device, not as a shared Firestore receipt. Dates are illustrative, not future-dated; monthly periods use the current and previous month.

Sample entries affect balances, dashboards, reports and backups, and can sync to members of a team workspace. Loading and clearing require confirmation and a writable, confirmed workspace; neither is available to a reviewer or a workspace locked for recovery. A workspace, access or record change during confirmation cancels the operation. Only one set of samples can be loaded at a time.

**Clear sample data** removes marked sample records, including edits to those marked records. It never cascades into unmarked records. If an unmarked transaction, payment, allocation, contributor, refund, activity or handover depends on sample data, a sample payment has an unmarked handover, a sample record has a pending request, or a sample refund contains unmarked non-cash credits, clearing is blocked until the dependency is resolved. Clearing is also blocked when the remaining core records would fail financial validation. Back up your records before resolving dependencies. An unmarked historical audit entry is retained. Save failures are reported; follow the storage warning and export the current data before reloading.

Sample loading does not create team accounts, invitations, member refund inbox requests, shared Firestore receipt documents, Faculty Fund recovery records, public reports, Storage objects or historical/orphaned records. It is not a complete Firestore deletion test or a substitute for an administrative inventory. Never use fictional entries as real financial records.
